# STONEVOW

### The Rime Wards, Book One

*Iona Rask*

---

*For everyone who has been someone else's warmth, and wanted, once, to be kept.*

---

**Content notes:** This book contains fantasy violence and injury, magical body horror (cold-sickness), on-page sexual content, grief and bereavement, and off-page character deaths. Reader discretion advised.

---

The wards hold because someone pays for them. That is the whole of the
arrangement: a wardwright to shape the stone, a hearth to feed it, and a
name read aloud at the binding so the village knows whose funeral to
expect.

I have set forty-one wards. I buried the man who taught me and the
woman who kept him warm, and I have never once asked for a hearth of
my own.

Then the rite named him.

An orchard-keeper who volunteered. Who traded his own body for his
sister's exemption. Who treats the cold like a rumour, and me like a
person, rather than an office.

Three months of winter. Forty miles of failing cairns. And a bond
that lets me feel, precisely, exactly how much of him the stone is
taking.

I did not choose this. I am no longer certain I would undo it if I
could.
