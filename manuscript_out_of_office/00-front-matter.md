# OUT OF OFFICE

### Kelvin Row, Book One

**Wren Halliday**

---

*For the people who keep the lights on in buildings nobody funds.*

---

**Content notes:** on-page grief for a parent · closed-door sexual content
· alcohol use · a past relationship made public without consent.

---

I tell the funding panel we have a couples hour. Live, Thursdays, six
weeks, an actual segment with an actual couple in it, the words out of my
mouth before I've checked whether either of those things is true.

Neither is. The roof leaks in three places I can name by bucket size. The
only person in the building both discreet enough and present enough to
fake a relationship with me is the overnight engineer, who has said
roughly eleven words to me in three years, four of which were about a
cable.

She says yes anyway, with conditions, written on the back of a cue sheet
in block capitals like a technical spec.

Six weeks. Six broadcasts. A kiss that's scripted and a kiss that isn't.
A secret she's kept off the record for two years and a promise I make her
before either of us knows what it will cost.

Somewhere around week four, live, on air, a caller asks how we knew we
could trust each other completely, and neither of us reaches for the
scripted answer.

We have two broadcasts left. I am running out of format.
