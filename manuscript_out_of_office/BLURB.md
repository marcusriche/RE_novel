# Out of Office

**Kelvin Row, Book One**
*Wren Halliday*

---

**She promised the funding panel a couple. She had six weeks and one very reluctant sound engineer.**

Here is what I say to the funding panel, out loud, with my whole chest: *yes, of course we have a new couples hour. Six weeks, live, Thursdays.*

Here is what I actually have at the point of saying it: a community radio station with a leaking roof, a volunteer producer who is seventeen, and Nadia — who engineers the overnight slot, and has said roughly eleven words to me in three years, four of them about a cable.

She agrees. That's the first surprise.

She's funny on air, warm in a way I never once clocked through three years of studio glass. That's the second.

And somewhere around week four, with the phone lines open and a stranger asking how we knew we were right for each other, I realise I've stopped rehearsing the answer.

We have two broadcasts left before the renewal decides everything. I'm running out of script — and I'm starting to suspect I don't want it back.

*Out of Office* is a slow-burn, fake-dating sapphic rom-com about two women who build something real by accident, on live radio, in front of an audience that was never supposed to matter as much as it does.

---

Six weeks. Six broadcasts. One relationship that exists entirely on air and nowhere else — until it starts existing everywhere else too. The funding panel wanted proof of community engagement. Nobody warned them what they would get.
