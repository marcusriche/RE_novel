# SAFE PASSAGE

### The Moretti Family, Book Two

**Sable Voss**

---

*For everyone still paying off a road they never chose.*

---

**Content notes:** organized crime and gun violence · explicit sexual
content · coercion and threats by third parties · human smuggling and
exploitation · an off-page death.

---

I have moved people the family wanted gone and people the family never
knew existed, and I have never once asked which was which. That is the
price of the passage they bought me at nineteen, and this week they came
to collect it.

One man. One route. Nineteen days to the border, and orders not to
deviate.

Except the man in my passenger seat is a federal witness with a date in
court, the route runs through three yards where nobody expects him to
arrive breathing, and somewhere between the lake and the line I stopped
counting him as cargo.

I can deliver him or I can keep him.

There is no manifest for both.
